# AbroadYield launch film: voice-over script

9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 76 seconds. Composition `AbroadYieldLaunch` (sources in
`src/launch/`). Music: the same track as the 45-second promo (same synth, tempo, key and chords),
re-cut to this edit by `scripts/soundtrack-launch.py`.

The narration is in English, like the product. Each line below is one shot: the shot lasts as long
as its line, so the real recording re-times the whole film (picture, captions and music).

## Direction for the voice

- Warm, confident, premium. Close to the mic, no hype: think "private banker who tells you the
  truth", not "infomercial".
- Pace about 2.8 words per second, with a short beat at each full stop.
- Punch the gold words (in **bold**): they are the ones that appear on screen, word by word.
- ElevenLabs settings that suit this read: Stability about 40%, Similarity about 75%, Style about
  35%, Speaker boost on. A deep, calm narrator voice (for example "Adam" or "Brian").

## Facts on screen

Every figure comes from the site: the Portugal example on the landing page ($250,000 listed at 7%,
4.3% after running costs, 3.1% and $7,856 after taxes, for a married couple in New York earning
$200k, all cash), the engine's ranking for that profile (Dubai 78, Panama 74, Thailand 72, Ireland
70, Japan 70, Costa Rica 64, Greece 62), the home stats (17 destinations, 51 state tax codes, 4 tax
layers, 1,200+ dated data points) and the pricing ($0, $39, $89 for 90 days, 14-day refund). The
seed data is still "sample data, to be verified", and the film says so in small print.

## The voice in this cut

The current narration is synthesized offline with Kokoro (open-weights TTS, voice `af_heart`, US
English) by `scripts/voice-launch.py`, which also writes the measured timings. It is a stand-in
for the ElevenLabs take: the environment has an `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, but its network policy
blocks `api.elevenlabs.io`.

## Replacing it with ElevenLabs (or a recorded voice)

Send either the 19 files named as above, or one single recording of the full text. Then:

1. Put the files in `public/launch/voice/` (a single take is split at the pauses).
2. Write each line's word start times (seconds from the clip start) and end in
   `src/launch/vo-timings.generated.ts`, and set `VOICE_READY = true`. Word timings can come from
   ElevenLabs' "with timestamps" output or from a transcription.
3. `bun scripts/timeline-launch.ts && python3 scripts/soundtrack-launch.py` (the music follows the
   new edit), then render. The music ducks by about 10 dB under each line.

## Render

```bash
cd video/abroadyield-promo && npm install
bun scripts/timeline-launch.ts
python3 scripts/soundtrack-launch.py
sh scripts/fetch-assets.sh
npx remotion render AbroadYieldLaunch out/abroadyield-launch.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=18
```
