# AbroadYield data audit — October 2026

Research audit of every tax rule, rate and market figure in the seed data, the US tax engine and
the site copy, run on 18 October 2026. It is **not** a professional sign-off: every figure stays
labelled _SAMPLE DATA – TO BE VERIFIED_ until a qualified person (US CPA / enrolled agent and
local counsel) reviews it in the admin. Sources were consulted through web search summaries;
direct page fetching was unavailable, so recheck a cited page before relying on it.

Status used below: **corrected** (applied, with source), **confirmed** (checked, unchanged),
**open** (not verified yet — the 200-searches-per-turn budget ran out).

## 4. Countries

### Portugal

- **Corrected** — IMT: flat **7.5%** for buyers who are not Portuguese tax residents (Decree-Law 97/2026, from May 2026; idealista, HVR). Capital gains: since 2023, 50% of the gain taxed at progressive rates, no buyer withholding (IFLR1000). Rental note: 25% on net long-term rent, interest not deductible. Lisbon: AL licences in containment zones lapse on sale (Hostaway). Prices (idealista, Sept 2026): Lisbon €6,250, Porto €4,300, Faro & Algarve €4,000, Braga €2,400, Funchal €4,050 per m².
- **Confirmed** — 25% rental rate, US treaty, Golden Visa property route ended, gross yield range.
- **Open** — taxable share for short-term (AL) income (believed 35% of gross), IMI (0.35% may be high; 0.25% suggested), financing, nightly rates and occupancy.

### Spain

- **Corrected** — National tourist-rental registry annulled by the Supreme Court (STS 620/2026, 19 May 2026); 3/5 owners' approval for new tourist lets since April 2025. ITP high end 13% (Catalonia). Notes on imputed income tax for unlet homes and the July 2025 refund ruling. Madrid (Plan Reside), Valencia (caps replaced the moratorium), Málaga (citywide 3-year moratorium from July 2026), Alicante (moratorium to January 2027; now _restricted_). Prices (idealista): Madrid €6,450, Barcelona €5,450, Valencia €3,450 (rent €1,300), Alicante €2,700.
- **Confirmed** — 24% on gross for non-EU owners, 19% CGT with 3% buyer withholding, US treaty, Golden Visa abolished, Barcelona 2028 phase-out.
- **Open** — notary/registry (0.5–1% may be closer than 1–1.5%), non-EU LTV (0.6 safer), nightly rates and occupancy, Marbella rent.

### Italy

- **Corrected** — Flat tax note: 21% (10% canone concordato); short lets 21% on one unit, 26% on others; **3+ short-let units count as a business from 2026** (L. 199/2025). Capital gains: 26% option within 5 years, Superbonus 10-year rule. Florence: _restricted_ (not banned) with outer zones being added; Milan keybox ban (Jan 2026).
- **Confirmed** — 21% / 26% rates, CIN mandatory since 2025.
- **Open** — registration tax range (9% on cadastral value), IMU, financing, Rome and Bologna local rules, all city prices and rents.

### Greece

- **Corrected** — Rental tax scale from 2026 income: **15% / 25% / 35% / 45%** (new 25% bracket, Law 5246/2025); electronic payment needed for the 5% deduction. Capital gains suspension ends **31 Dec 2026**. Golden Visa €800k / €400k (€250k only for conversions/restorations), no short lets for Golden Visa homes. Athens: AMA freeze in districts 1–3, registrations lapse on sale, climate fee.
- **Confirmed** — 3.09% transfer tax, 5% flat deduction.
- **Open** — Thessaloniki restrictions, ENFIA approximation, financing, city figures.

### Mexico

- **Corrected** — Tax note (25% on gross or 35% net with a representative; 16% VAT and lodging tax on short stays). ISAI high end 5% (Mexico City table). Playa del Carmen rent 15,000 MXN (was 20,000). San Miguel de Allende 42,000 MXN/m² (was 55,000), Tulum 45,000.
- **Confirmed** — Art. 158 LISR, fideicomiso zones, platform withholding, Mexico City registry and 50% rule, capital gains, US treaty, Mexico City / Puerto Vallarta / Los Cabos prices.
- **Open** — residency thresholds after the July 2025 guidelines, financing, short-term figures, Quintana Roo registry, Mérida price.

### Costa Rica

- **Corrected** — Investor residency: the US$150k route (Law 9996) **expired 14 July 2026**; Rentista US$2,500/month. Capital gains: 2.25% option applies to property bought **before** July 2019, first sale only; 2.5% buyer withholding for non-resident sellers. Law 9742: all short-term rentals register with the ICT. Registry/stamps 0.5–0.8%. Luxury home tax added to notes.
- **Confirmed** — 15% on 85% of gross, 13% VAT on lodging, 1.5% transfer tax, 0.25% property tax, no US treaty, maritime-zone 49% rule.
- **Open** — all city figures, financing, notary fee incl. VAT.

### United Arab Emirates (Dubai)

- **Corrected** — Since April 2026 the 2-year investor visa has no minimum value for sole owners of completed property (NTL). Regulator renamed DET (formerly DTCM) everywhere; permit, insurance, lock and Tourism Dirham details.
- **Confirmed** — 4% DLD fee, trustee fees, mortgage registration 0.25%, LTV 50–60%, rates, service charges, no income or capital gains tax (Cabinet Decision 49/2023), AED peg, Marina / Downtown / Business Bay prices and rents.
- **Open** — JVC price (14,000 AED/m² may be more current), short-term occupancy (2026 weakness), gross yield range top (9% for JVC).

### France

- **Corrected** — IRS accepts CSG/CRDS as creditable (IRS practice unit, 2025); NIIT not offset. Capital gains detail (19% + 17.2%, surtax, 22/30-year allowances, fiscal representative). **Paris: 90 nights** since 2025 (was 120). Loi Le Meur energy rule.
- **Confirmed** — 20% minimum rate + 17.2% social charges, micro-foncier 30%, DMTO 5.81–6.32%.
- **Open** — furnished (LMNP) regime not modelled, notary fees, financing, taxe foncière, all city figures, Bordeaux/Montpellier rules.

### United Kingdom

- **Corrected** — US citizens get **no personal allowance**: 20% band now starts at £0 (USD 50,141 / 166,436 thresholds). Rates rise to 22/42/47% from April 2027. Scotland: LBTT + 8% ADS (affects Edinburgh). Renters' Rights Act in force (May 2026). Edinburgh control area and 5% visitor levy.
- **Confirmed** — SDLT surcharges (5% + 2%), NRL scheme, CGT 18%/24% with 60-day reporting, London 90 nights.
- **Open** — city prices and rents (London average may be lower), financing.

### Ireland

### Germany

### Croatia

### Panama
